# Lessons Learned

## Visibility & Atmosphere
- **Rely on Natural 2D Lighting Rather Than Mesh Fog of War Overlays**: The user prefers the game's atmosphere and visibility to come from the URP 2D lighting setup (dark factory rooms, steady/flickering hallway lights, and dynamic muzzle flashes) rather than a procedural Fog of War mesh overlay. Do not re-introduce a Fog of War mesh unless explicitly requested.

## Map Generation & Doorways
- **Doorway Swing Clearance & Obstruction Prevention**: Doors must never spawn against, swing into, or be obstructed by interior cover pillars, perpendicular wall jambs, or dead-end geometry. Always enforce generous clearance boxes (at least 4 tiles / full door length + margins) around all doorway openings and hinges to guarantee door swing arcs and player passage remain 100% unobstructed. Re-validate door clearances post-instantiation.
- **Arterial Hallway Skeleton in Ringless BSP Layouts**: When eliminating a central hub or ring hallway, ensure the building retains an arterial corridor network (such as flanking corridors connecting stairwell throats and entrance avenues) carved prior to interior BSP room placement. This guarantees that all entrances and multi-floor stairwells remain traversable, and allows BSP rooms to reliably find adjacent hallway anchors for doorways.
- **Dynamic Objective Placement & Non-Square Aspect Ratios**: Footprints should not be constrained to square dimensions. Generate width and height independently per seed within configurable ranges, and elect the Objective Hub dynamically from the largest qualifying BSP leaf partition on the objective floor rather than hardcoding it to the map center.
- **Door Placement, Wall Continuity & Isolated Pillar Prevention**: Doors must never spawn attached to isolated 1x1 pillars, freestanding wall blocks, or blind openings. Both door jambs must have verified solid wall continuity (at least one neighboring wall/window cell excluding the doorway opening itself), guaranteeing doors strictly punctuate genuine room perimeters or corridors. Never synthesize synthetic 1x1 wall pillars in open floor to mount a door. Direct room-to-room doorways across short gaps must only be carved when rooms share a single dividing wall (`dist == 1`) to eliminate redundant double back-to-back doors. Hallway widening passes must respect jamb continuity and not breach perimeter walls or isolate jamb blocks.

## Multi-Floor & Objective Interactions
- **Multi-Floor Vertical Isolation for 2D Interacts**: Because all floors share overlapping 2D $(x, y)$ world coordinates, 2D distance checks (`Vector2.Distance(player, target) <= radius`) will evaluate as in-range even if the player is standing on the floor directly above or below the interactable object. All world interactions, objective terminals, extraction zones, and trigger volumes must explicitly validate that the player's active floor matches the object's floor on both client and server authority before displaying interaction prompts, allowing input, or accepting progress RPCs. Hide world-space UI/progress overlays when on mismatched floors.

## Team Spawns & Spatial Separation
- **Prevent Corner Clashing & Premature Team Combat**: When selecting equidistant team entrance candidates across different cardinal walls, a pure travel-time delta minimizer naturally clusters spawns and doors around the same 90-degree corner because corner candidates have near-identical Manhattan/Euclidean distances to a central objective room. Always enforce Euclidean spatial separation checks across all candidate pairs: exterior team spawns ($\ge 45\text{f}$ units or $0.45\times \min(\text{width}, \text{height})$), perimeter entrance doors ($\ge 30\text{f}$ units or $0.30\times \min(\text{width}, \text{height})$), and interior corridor breach points ($\ge 18\text{f}$ units or $0.20\times \min(\text{width}, \text{height})$). Use a multi-tier search (full separation $\to$ relaxed separation $\to$ unconstrained fallback) and verify all separation metrics post-generation with public assertions (`AllTeamSpawnsSeparatedVerified`).

## Stairwells & Elevation Transitions
- **Floor-Dependent Wall Masking & Solid Handrail Enclosures**: In 2D top-down multi-floor maps, stairs should not allow players to walk underneath elevated step flights or drop off edges. Instead of complex elevation shadow masks or under-stair collision switching, permanently enclose stair flights with continuous solid handrails and spawn solid concrete walls masking the inoperable throat side on each floor:
  - On the `LowerFloor`, open only the bottom landing entrance into the corridor (e.g. $y \in [\text{BayMinY}, \text{BayMinY} + 3]$), while carving a solid concrete wall covering the upper portion ($y \in [\text{BayMinY} + 4, \text{BayMinY} + 9]$) so the side beneath the stairs is completely inaccessible.
  - On the `UpperFloor`, carve a solid concrete wall covering the lower drop-off portion ($y \in [\text{BayMinY}, \text{BayMinY} + 5]$), leaving only the top landing ($y \in [\text{BayMinY} + 6, \text{BayMinY} + 9]$) open into the corridor.
  - In post-processing passes (such as hallway widening or dead-end notch trimming), guard stair bay cells with protected zones so automated cleanups never punch holes through stairwell wall enclosures or seal landing mouths.
  - Space staircase candidate slots vertically with sufficient margins (e.g. $\ge 7$ tiles clearance between Top, Mid, and Bottom bays) to prevent distinct floor stairwell shafts from overlapping in 2D space.

## Map Exploration & Hallway Flow
- **Corridor Grid Continuity & Dead-End Hallway Elimination**: Hallways that lead to nothing disorient players and disrupt pacing. Arterial corridors must form closed loops spanning the building footprint, with every corridor terminating at another corridor intersection, stairwell landing bay, or entrance vestibule. An iterative dead-end hallway stub pruning pass (`PruneDeadEndHallwayStubs`) must trim any dead-end corridor segments slice-by-slice back to the nearest doorway or corridor intersection while strictly protecting entrance vestibules, stair landing bays, and doorway clearance zones.
- **Wall-to-Wall BSP Partitioning & Closet Minimization**: Random closet placement and undersized BSP fragments clutter the layout and dilute exploration. Sectors bounded by arterial corridors must be subdivided via BSP into spacious standard, workshop, or auditorium rooms packed wall-to-wall. Utility closets must be strictly capped at $\le 1$ per floor (`floorClosetCount <= 1`), with fallback random closet placement completely eliminated.
- **Multi-Door Flow-Through Connectivity**: To promote seamless exploration and tactical rotation, 100% of standard, workshop, and auditorium rooms must have $\ge 2$ doorways. Direct room-to-room doorways across shared dividing walls must support both 1-tile and 2-tile dividing walls. All intermediate wall tiles must be converted to `CellType.Doorway` and designated as a single swing door pair, and doorway deduplication passes must be non-destructive (marking duplicates handled without reverting tiles to `CellType.Wall`). Exterior entrance corridors must maintain a 1-tile solid concrete wall buffer from all interior rooms to avoid clipping into room walls or destroying door jambs.
- **Central Objective Room Placement for Fair Multi-Team Travel**: Sorting candidate BSP leaves with an outer loop over descending dimension (`minDim`) causes distant corner leaves to be chosen over central leaves simply because they are slightly larger (e.g. 14x14 in a corner vs 12x12 in the center). Corner placement creates severe travel time asymmetry across multi-team perimeter spawns. Objective candidate selection must prioritize proximity to the factory center `Vector2(_width * 0.5f, _height * 0.5f)` across all qualifying leaves ($\ge 10\times 10$), ensuring balanced travel times from all 4 perimeter walls.
